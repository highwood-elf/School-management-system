Our Story

The story of FRAGLUXE began in 1894 within a quiet, sun-drenched estate in the hills of Provence. Founded by master botanist Jean-Luc Vaneau, the house originally crafted bespoke botanical elixirs for European royalty, utilizing cold-extraction methods and wild-harvested flora from his private conservatory.


For over a century, the house operated on a simple truth: nature holds an irreplaceable genius. However, as the mid-20th century saw the luxury industry shift toward synthetic mass-production and chemical shortcuts, the Vaneau family chose a different path. Rather than expanding into industrial manufacturing, they quietly shuttered the atelier to preserve the integrity of their formulas.


Three generations later, the family archives were reopened. Modernizing historic 19th-century formulas with cutting-edge green chemistry, [Brand Name] was reborn. Today, we merge over a hundred years of artisanal heritage with forward-thinking ecological stewardship—delivering the same uncompromising prestige, rooted in a deep reverence for the Earth.

Our Mission

Our mission is to pioneer a new era of high perfumery—one where uncompromising luxury and total ecological responsibility coexist without exception.We exist to prove that the world’s most exquisite fragrances can be reated in complete harmony with nature. By pairing centuries-old French craftsmanship with zero-waste bio-technology, we are committed to Restoring ecosystems through regenerative agriculture and ethical sourcing, Redefining prestige by replacing synthetic filler with pure, fully traceable botanicals, Eliminating environmental impact across every stage of production, from seed to bottle. We don't just craft fine fragrance; we safeguard the natural world that inspires it.

Sourcing Ingredients

he soul of a fragrance lies in the integrity of its origin, and we believe the world’s most captivating notes should never come at the expense of the ecosystems that yield them. We partner directly with small-scale, regenerative farms and fair-trade cooperatives across the globe—sourcing hand-harvested Vetiver from Haiti and sustainable Damask Rose from the valleys of Bulgaria. Every botanical oil, absolute, and essence in our library is fully traceable back to its native soil, ensuring non-exploitative labor and equitable living wages for the farming communities who nurture these precious crops.

Where natural harvesting threatens endangered species, such as Sandalwood or Rosewood, we refuse to deplete fragile wild habitats. Instead, we champion green science innovations, utilizing bio-identical, zero-waste extraction methods to replicate these rare olfactory profiles without felling a single tree. By marrying ethical agriculture with modern scientific restraint, we preserve the purity of high perfumery while honoring the delicate balance of the natural world.

Footprint

True luxury is reflected not only in the final creation, but in the trace it leaves behind. Every bottle of FRAGLUXE is housed in a heavy, custom-crafted vessel of recyclable forest-green glass, topped with a hand-turned wooden cap sourced exclusively from FSC-certified sustainable forests. We have completely eliminated single-use plastics throughout our supply chain, encasing our bottles in secondary packaging made from 100% post-consumer recycled paper and finished with non-toxic, biodegradable vegetable inks.

Our commitment to ecological accountability extends deep into our manufacturing process. From energy-efficient cold-pressed extractions in our solar-powered laboratory to localized, low-emission distribution networks, every step is designed to minimize environmental disruption. The result is a fully certified carbon-neutral lifecycle from seed to bottle—ensuring that your signature scent is an indulgence both exquisite and entirely guilt-free.

The art of the Scent

Formulating a luxury fragrance is an exercise in patient alchemy, where time is treated as an essential ingredient. Unlike mass-produced perfumes aged for mere days in industrial vats, each [Brand Name] creation undergoes an extended maceration process in small batches, allowing raw natural oils and delicate absolutes to harmonize organically. The result is a multi-layered composition that evolves seamlessly on the skin throughout the day. Opening with vibrant top notes, shifting into complex botanical hearts, and settling into rich, intimate base notes, our scents are designed not to overpower a room, but to leave an intriguing, unforgettable trail that is unique to the wearer’s natural body chemistry.


Sustainable Innovation

We believe the future of sustainable luxury lies at the intersection of nature’s intelligence and human ingenuity. Through pioneering bio-technological extractions, we capture scent molecules from living plants without harming them—a process known as "headspace technology"—allowing us to recreate the fresh, authentic scent of rare night-blooming flowers or rainforest mosses with zero ecological disruption. By replacing petroleum-derived solvents with upcycled plant-based alternatives and utilizing closed-loop water systems in our laboratory, we push the boundaries of modern olfactory art while maintaining an uncompromising standard of environmental stewardship.
